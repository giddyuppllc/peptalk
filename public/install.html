Install PepTalk

Add PepTalk to your phone — it opens as its own app, no browser.

Don’t see a prompt? Add it in 2 taps:
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right of Chrome).
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
Then open PepTalk from your Home screen — it runs fullscreen as its own app.

Add PepTalk to your Home Screen

iPhone & iPad install PepTalk straight from Safari:

  1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — PepTalk lands on your Home Screen.

Open in Safari to install

On iPhone & iPad, PepTalk installs from Safari. Copy this page’s link and open it in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
