The uncomfortable part: the least attributable source drives the most behaviour. protocols.ts is imported by 11 modules to the master table's 3 — including the code that decides whether a logged dose counts as an overdose.

And "the table always wins" cannot be a blanket rule. NAD+ proves it: the ladder doses 60 mg, the table says 200–600 mcg. Exactly 100× out — a transcription slip, not a difference of clinical opinion.
