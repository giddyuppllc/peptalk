PepTalk · clinical data workbench

Everything PepTalk asserts, in one pass.

Four things need a clinician: Dose and Cycle, where the app stores the same figure in several places and they disagree; Safety, where 64 of 79 compounds ship with no contraindications, adverse effects or pregnancy note at all; and Interactions, where the app suggests 139 peptide pairings but holds data on 25. Blank means nothing is on file — not zero, not safe. Click a cell to accept that figure, or type the right one. Red is a gap or a disagreement; green agrees but still needs your sign-off. Nothing here changes the app — answers are collected so the code can be corrected deliberately.
